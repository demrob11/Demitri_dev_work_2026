Guidelines for using AI safely
-------------------------------

# always remember your intention behind your action

# realize your strengths by doing and reflecting

# never seek the path of laziness, instead seek the path of understanding

-------------------------------


Why using Artificial Intelligence can be dangerous
---------------------------------------------------

Sometimes, think that the "path" to discovery and realization can be misleading. To "learn" is to understand; I think that sometimes taking the easy path or the "lazy way" can create gaps in understanding which may not be fully understood, and could have unintended consequences.
